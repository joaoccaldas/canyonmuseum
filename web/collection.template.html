Published dimensions

Each exhibit has a source snapshot, size-specific geometry, separate components and a repeatable build. Model pivots are checked against Canyon; unseen surface depths remain reconstructed.

Research & remaining gaps ↗

Fit before speed

Measured segments drive the human avatar. Static joint checks and 27 candidate positions help explore contact geometry. Comfort, dynamic motion, pressure and sustainable power need real rider measurements.

Physics with provenance

Force, power and time follow stated equations. Imported yaw curves are tied to a setup. Flow trails are illustrative; pressure maps and manufacturer-specific gains require validated measurements or CFD.
